How to play

Survive every wave. Place towers anywhere off the path and out of the lake — there's no grid, just click where you want it.

Turret — $400, shoots. Oil Derrick — $200, earns money. Car Depot — $350, sends a jeep out from your base every 45s to ram enemies head-on. All have 4 upgrades.
1 turret  ·  2 derrick  ·  3 car depot  ·  R rotate while placing  ·  Z cancel  ·  E upgrade  ·  X sell
